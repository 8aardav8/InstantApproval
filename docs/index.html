✅ Benefits of Ownership

As long as you fulfill your end of the installment sale agreement and make all payments on time, the seller cannot end the purchase contract for this home (like a landlord can with a lease). You are securing a stable house payment with no rent increases going forward, and you can do anything you want with/in the house, as long as it's legal.

✅ You Have Options!

You can pay the home off early, sell, or refinance, and you can rent it out to others if you want. If you ever want to cancel the agreement, you can do so at any time with no further obligation, if you are paid current. You would just sign a termination agreement, leave the house broom clean, and return the keys.

⚠️ Taxes & Insurance

Property taxes and homeowners' insurance must be paid monthly to protect both buyer and seller.
Taxes are included in your monthly payment, but insurance is your responsibility. Be prepared to take ownership of this property and cover these expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is seller financing?

Instead of getting a bank mortgage, you make payments directly to the seller over time -- typically a 30-year term, just like a normal bank mortgage. Once your down payment is made, you're automatically approved. No applications, no credit checks.

Do I need good credit or a credit check?

No. There's no application and no credit check required.

What do I need to buy a home?

Just four things: a phone number, an email, a valid ID for each buyer, and the down payment received in full.

How do payments work?

Payments are due on the 1st of the month and typically paid online. Property taxes are included in your monthly payment; homeowners' insurance is your own responsibility.

Can I see a property in person before deciding?

Yes -- drive by any available property anytime to see the street, the outside, and look in the windows. If you're still interested, call or text us at 618-418-4180 to schedule an interior walkthrough.

Do I need an inspection?

An inspection is highly recommended before committing. All homes are sold as-is, so you'll want a professional to look at the property's condition and discuss any needed work with you first.

How do I reserve a property?

You pay 50% of the down payment as a non-refundable deposit. Once that's received, marketing on the property stops and it's reserved for you while paperwork is sent for e-signature.

What happens after I reserve a property?

After you e-sign the documents, you pay any remaining down payment. Once the full down payment is received and we sign, the home is yours -- you can get insurance, do occupancy inspections, connect utilities, start repairs, or even move in the same day.

Can I pay the home off early, sell, or refinance?

Yes to all three. You can also rent the property out to others if you want.

What if I need to cancel the agreement?

As long as you're paid current, you can cancel at any time with no further obligation -- just sign a termination agreement, leave the house broom clean, and return the keys.

What condition are the homes sold in?

As-is. You're responsible for any inspections and repairs needed at your new property.

Can I browse homes that are Pending or already Sold?

Yes -- the Properties page defaults to showing Available homes, but you can use the Availability filter to also see Pending and Sold listings.

A property's photo link isn't working -- what do I do?

On that property's page, tap "Photo link not working?" -- it sends us a quick text so we can fix it.

How do I share a listing with someone else?

Tap "Share" on any property's page -- it opens a text message pre-filled with the property details and photo link, ready to send to whoever you'd like.
