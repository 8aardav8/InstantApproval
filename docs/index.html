✅ Benefits of Ownership

As long as you fulfill your end of the installment sale agreement and make all payments on time, the seller cannot end the purchase contract for this home (like a landlord can with a lease). You are securing a stable house payment with no rent increases going forward, and you can do anything you want with/in the house, as long as it's legal.

✅ You Have Options!

You can pay the home off early, sell, or refinance, and you can rent it out to others if you want. If you ever want to cancel the agreement, you can do so at any time with no further obligation, if you are paid current. You would just sign a termination agreement, leave the house broom clean, and return the keys.
